# HerNext

## Subtitle

Turn a nonlinear path into one credible story and one realistic next step.

## Project description

HerNext is a focused planning tool for women returning to work,
changing careers, or pursuing a first formal role after a nonlinear path.

Career platforms usually ask for continuous employment, formal titles, and a
perfect résumé. They rarely recognize the skills demonstrated through personal
projects, caregiving, community work, independent study, or managing competing
responsibilities. Generic action plans can create another barrier by assuming
that every candidate has unlimited uninterrupted time.

HerNext starts somewhere more honest. The user describes her target
direction, her path, the time she really has each week, and proof points she can
support with examples. The product then creates:

- a professional positioning statement grounded in her own words;
- a small one-week plan with effort estimates and tangible deliverables;
- transparent opportunity matches with plain-language reasons; and
- a progress workspace where the user completes steps and chooses one focused
  application.

The product does not decide whether someone belongs. It makes evidence visible
and leaves the consequential choice with the user.

## Target audience and pain point

The first user is a woman returning to professional growth after caregiving,
study, migration, unemployment, or another life transition. She may have strong
project experience but no recent formal title, and only three to ten focused
hours each week. Her challenge is not a lack of potential; it is translating a
nonlinear path into credible evidence and a plan that fits real life.

## What makes it different

1. **Time-aware by design.** The action plan changes with actual weekly
   availability instead of producing an unrealistic checklist.
2. **Evidence before scoring.** Projects, self-learning, coordination, and
   community work become concrete proof points.
3. **Explainable recommendations.** Each match states why it fits; no opaque
   system decides eligibility or worth.
4. **Focused execution.** The prototype leads to one story, one proof asset, and
   one reviewed application rather than an overwhelming job-search dashboard.

## Core functionality

- Editable demo story for immediate evaluation.
- Validated HerNext brief with goal, time, story, and proof points.
- Deterministic, explainable planning engine.
- Generated positioning statement with copy action.
- Three-step weekly route with completion tracking.
- Synthetic opportunity matches with visible selection state.
- Responsive English interface with keyboard and reduced-motion support.
- No login and no personal-data transmission.

## Demand and path forward

The product addresses a common return-to-work pattern: people often have useful
experience that is difficult to express in the format expected by employers and
opportunity providers. The next validation step is five moderated tests with
returning women, measuring whether they can create a credible story and choose a
next step in under ten minutes.

A production version would add verified live opportunity feeds, an evidence
vault, multilingual support, and optional human mentor review. The individual
workspace would remain free for a limited number of plans. Universities,
workforce programs, and women-led communities could license cohort support.

## Development process and tools

This implementation was created from scratch during the Elevate Women Global
Hackathon 2026 using Next.js, React, TypeScript, CSS, Git, and AI-assisted
development with OpenAI Codex. Codex supported requirements analysis, ideation,
English copy, code generation, review, and testing. The participant directed the
product decisions, reviewed the outputs, and remains responsible for the work.

All candidate and opportunity examples are synthetic. The application uses no
third-party images, private datasets, or external personal information.

## Current limitations

- Opportunity listings are synthetic and used only to demonstrate the flow.
- The planner uses transparent local rules; it does not yet call an external AI
  model or live opportunity API.
- The prototype does not create or submit real applications.
- User testing with the intended audience is the next validation milestone.
