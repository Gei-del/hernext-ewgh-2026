# Demo video script — 2:40 maximum

Speak in Spanish if that is more comfortable and add the English subtitles below.
The screen recording should show the interface in English.

## 0:00–0:18 — Problem

**Voice:**

“A career path can pause or change shape. Potential does not. But most career
platforms recognize formal titles, not the projects, caregiving, and persistence
behind a nonlinear path.”

**Screen:** Hero and the three-stop route.

**English subtitle:** Same text as the voice.

## 0:18–0:35 — User and promise

**Voice:**

“ReStart Compass helps a woman turn the life she has into one credible
professional story and one realistic next step.”

**Screen:** Scroll to the ReStart brief and select **Load demo story**.

## 0:35–1:02 — Inputs grounded in real life

**Voice:**

“The user chooses her direction, explains what she kept building or learning,
and selects the hours she really has. Her projects and coordination work become
proof points, not an apology for a gap.”

**Screen:** Change six hours to three, then back to six. Select a proof point.

## 1:02–1:27 — Generate the plan

**Voice:**

“In one action, the product creates an explainable plan. It preserves her words,
estimates effort, and produces tangible deliverables that fit her availability.”

**Screen:** Select **Build my ReStart plan** and show the positioning statement.

## 1:27–1:52 — Functional workflow

**Voice:**

“Every control has a result. She can copy her positioning, complete each step,
see her progress, and choose one opportunity instead of facing an overwhelming
list.”

**Screen:** Copy the statement, complete two steps, and choose one match.

## 1:52–2:13 — Safety and honesty

**Voice:**

“This hackathon prototype sends no personal data anywhere. The opportunities are
clearly labeled synthetic, recommendations are explainable, and the final choice
always belongs to the user.”

**Screen:** Show the privacy sentence and synthetic-data note.

## 2:13–2:32 — Development process

**Voice:**

“I built this implementation from scratch during the hackathon with Next.js,
React, TypeScript, Git, and OpenAI Codex for AI-assisted planning, coding,
English copy, review, and testing.”

**Screen:** Briefly show the repository commit history, README, and passing build.

## 2:32–2:40 — Close

**Voice:**

“ReStart Compass does not decide whether a woman belongs. It helps her make the
evidence visible and move forward.”

**Screen:** Return to the completed route and product name.

## Recording checklist

- Keep the final video at 2:40–2:50, never above three minutes.
- Record at 1080p with browser zoom at 100%.
- Hide bookmarks, personal tabs, email, notifications, and private information.
- Add English captions if the spoken narration is not fully in English.
- Test the public video link in an incognito window before submitting.
