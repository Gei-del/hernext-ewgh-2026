# HerNext product brief

## Problem

Women returning after caregiving, study, migration, or another life transition
often have strong evidence of learning and delivery, but opportunity systems read
only formal titles and continuous timelines. Generic career advice also assumes
more uninterrupted time than many people actually have.

## User

The first user is a woman returning to professional growth with project or
community experience, a target direction, and between three and ten focused
hours available each week.

## Promise

HerNext turns the user’s own story and proof points into a professional
positioning statement, a small one-week action plan, and a transparent set of
synthetic opportunity matches. The user chooses the opportunity and marks her
own progress.

## Scope

- Client-side prototype; no account and no external data transfer.
- Explainable planning rules instead of an opaque eligibility decision.
- Synthetic opportunities clearly labeled in the interface.
- AI-assisted development is disclosed; generated output remains human-reviewed.

## Success

- A judge can complete the core flow in under two minutes.
- Every control has a real visible result.
- The generated work fits within the selected weekly availability.
- The application remains usable on a narrow mobile viewport.
